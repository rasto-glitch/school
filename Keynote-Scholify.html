Pillar 01
Academy.
A personal note
“Before I built Scholify,
I was a teacher.”
My students understood a topic perfectly in class — and a week later it was gone. So I wrote the lessons in my own words, online, the way I'd explain them in class. Their recall improved. Their grades went up. That experience is baked into every part of Scholify's academy.
