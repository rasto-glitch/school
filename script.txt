Introducing Scholify

No more juggling between tools. No more information falling through the cracks. No more parents left in the dark.
Every school runs on three things: Academy, Management, and Accounting. Scholify was built around all three.

Academy
I want to tell you something personal.
Before I built Scholify, I was a teacher. And there was a problem I couldn't shake.
My students would understand a topic perfectly inside the classroom. They'd answer questions, engage, follow along. But the next week? It was as if someone had erased everything. The knowledge just didn't stick.
I didn't think it was the students' fault. I thought about how they learn best — and I realized: they understand their teacher far better than they understand their textbook. So I built them a simple website, where I wrote explanations in my own words, the same way I'd explain things in class.
The results surprised me. Days, even weeks later, students were recalling topics in detail. Their exam grades went up. Something as simple as giving them access to their teacher's voice — outside the classroom — made a real difference.
That experience is baked into every part of Scholify's academy system.
Homework & Assignments
Teachers can post homework and assignments directly inside the platform. Set due dates, attach images, add supporting materials — all in one place. No more WhatsApp groups. No more parents accidentally having a teacher's personal phone number. The process is streamlined, and everyone's privacy is protected.
Learn: E-Books & Teacher Blogs
Scholify's Learn feature gives students two powerful tools. First, a digital library where they can access their curriculum and any additional books the school makes available. Second, a built-in blog platform where teachers can post explanations, breakdowns, and notes in their own words — the same way they'd explain something in class.
Students revisit lessons on their own time, in their teacher's voice. Knowledge sticks.
Parent-Teacher Communication
The best schools treat parents as partners. Scholify makes that partnership easy. Teachers can chat directly with parents about academic performance. Supervisors can reach out about behavioral concerns. Parents always have a clear, direct line to the people responsible for their child's day — without anyone's personal contact details being shared.
Monthly Reports
Every month, teachers can generate a structured report for each student — covering grades, classroom behavior, and personalized notes and suggestions. Parents stay informed in a meaningful way, not just at the end of the year when it may be too late to act.
End-of-Year Grades
When the year ends, parents receive their child's final grades directly inside the app. No printed papers. No waiting in line. Everything is there, clearly presented, the moment it's ready.

Management
Live Bus Tracking
A parent's first priority is knowing their child is safe. Scholify's live bus tracking gives them exactly that.
Parents can see their child's bus moving in real time on a map, along with a live estimated arrival time. Every relevant detail about the bus is available — bus number, license plate, and the driver's contact information. When the bus starts its route, parents are notified. When it's getting close, they're notified again.
This isn't a convenience feature. It's peace of mind — and it's the kind of thing that builds deep trust between a school and its families.
School Announcements
Holidays, sports days, science fairs, parent evenings — schools are full of events, and keeping everyone informed shouldn't be a logistical headache. With Scholify, announcements go out through the app instantly. Parents are notified, and they can view, like, and comment on each post. The school's community stays connected and engaged, without anyone having to manage a broadcast list.
Reception & Appointments
Your reception team handles a lot. Scheduling meetings, answering the same questions repeatedly, managing parent walk-ins. Scholify gives parents the ability to book appointments directly through the app, and reception can respond, confirm, and manage those appointments without the back-and-forth.
Common questions — tuition balances, upcoming events, required documents — can be answered through the platform, saving time on both sides of the conversation.
Student & Staff Records
Every student's records — name, date of birth, parent contact details, address, academic history, grades — stored securely and digitally. The same goes for your teaching and administrative staff. No more paper folders. No more searching through filing cabinets. Everything is organized, accessible, and protected.

Accounting
A school cannot fulfill its mission if it isn't financially healthy. And financial health requires clarity.
Tuition Management
Scholify tracks every tuition plan in the system — paid, unpaid, and overdue. Accountants can see the full picture at a glance. When a payment is coming due, the system can notify parents automatically, reducing late payments without awkward phone calls.
Adjustments and discounts can be applied directly in the platform. Both the accounting team and parents can access receipts and statements at any time. And if a family falls significantly behind, the school can restrict access to certain features — like monthly reports or grade viewing — until the account is settled.
Staff Salaries
Every teacher and staff member's salary is tracked inside Scholify. Payment schedules, amounts, and applicable taxes are all managed in one place. Staff are notified through the app when a payment is processed. Nothing falls through the cracks, and nothing requires a separate spreadsheet to track.
Full Financial Oversight
Scholify gives your accounting department the tools it needs to operate with complete visibility: ledgers, accounts receivable tracking, profit and loss reporting, cash flow management, and accounting period controls. Payment accounts — cash drawers, bank accounts, mobile wallets — are all managed within the system. Multi-currency support means consolidated reporting stays clean even across different payment types.
Your accounting team finally has what they've always needed: one place where everything is accounted for.
